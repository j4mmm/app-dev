#Bloodhounds
**Kdrama Series**
> Bloodhounds is a a 2023 action-thriller K-drama on Netflix about two young boxers, Gun-woo and Woo-jin, who team up to fight a brutal, greedy loan shark, Kim Myeong-gil, during the COVID-19 pandemic.
**Characters**
1. Kim Geon-woo
2. Hong Woo-jin
3. Kim Myeong-gil
4. President Choi
5. Im Baek-jeong
**Plot**
> Bloodhounds follows two young boxers, Gun-woo and Woo-jin, who partner with a benevolent moneylender to destroy a ruthless loan shark organization exploiting pandemic-stricken families. The action-thriller centers on their bromance and brutal fight scenes, focusing on loyalty, survival, and taking down corrupt financial predators.
